Meet the AI assistant

Beyond code lookups, AutoTrail can reason through a described problem the way a good mechanic would talk you through it — in plain language, with a next step. Here's a taste of what it sounds like.

"Car jerks when I accelerate, and check engine light just came on"
Likely cause: This pattern usually points to a misfire — often a worn spark plug or ignition coil rather than something major. Do this first: avoid hard acceleration until checked; ask your mechanic to scan for a misfire code before replacing anything.
"AC blows but not cold, especially in traffic"
Likely cause: Low refrigerant or a failing compressor clutch are the two most common causes of this exact pattern. Do this first: ask for a refrigerant pressure check — it's the cheaper fix to rule out before touching the compressor.
"Grinding sound when I brake, worse in the morning"
Likely cause: Worn brake pads are the most common explanation, but morning-only grinding can also mean surface rust on the rotor from overnight moisture. Do this first: have pad thickness checked — don't delay if the grinding is metal-on-metal.
